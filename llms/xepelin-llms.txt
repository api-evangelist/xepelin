# Xepelin

> Xepelin is a Chilean-founded fintech operating in Chile and Mexico that provides digital financial services for small and mid-sized businesses: invoice factoring (adelanto de facturas), supplier payment financing (confirming), working capital credit, collections and payments management, and XData, a financial-intelligence product for evaluating clients and suppliers. Xepelin does not currently publish a public developer API or developer portal.
